About RampartChess

Rampart Chess is a strategic hybrid of chess and card play. Two armies face each other across a rampart barrier, and victory comes by checkmating the enemy king - using both tactical piece movement and a deck of cards.

It's an independent project, built and maintained by one developer with help from a small group of contributors.
