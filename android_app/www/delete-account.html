Delete your RampartChess account

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in at rampartchess.com or in the Rampart Chess app.
  2. Open your Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Choose Delete my account..., type your username, enter your password, and confirm.

You can't delete an account while you have a two-player game in progress - finish or resign it first.

Can't sign in?

Email apecrank@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Rampart account" and your username. We'll delete it for you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and email address
  • Your profile: username (which becomes available to others), bio, country, avatar
  • Your trophies and rating history
  • Your friends list, friend requests and game challenges
  • Your private messages (the conversation is removed for both people)
  • Your games against the computer

What is kept

  • Finished games you played against other people, and the in-game chat from them, stay in those players' histories - but they no longer identify you. Your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and nothing links the games back to you.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It can't be undone.
