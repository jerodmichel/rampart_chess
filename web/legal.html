This is a plain-language starter policy for a small hobby project, not a lawyer-drafted legal document. It's meant to set honest expectations while RampartChess is in early testing - it should get a real legal review before any wide public launch.

Terms of Service

What this is

RampartChess is an independently-developed chess variant, currently in active development and testing. Features, rules, and data may change or reset without notice while it's in this state.

Accounts

An account requires a real email address (used for sign-in, password recovery, email verification, and the notification emails described in the Privacy Policy below) and a username you choose. You must be at least 13 years old. You're responsible for keeping your login credentials to yourself. You can delete your account at any time from the bottom of your Profile page; see how account deletion works for exactly what is removed and what is kept.

Conduct

Game chat and any public profile text (bio, username) should stay respectful. Harassment, hate speech, spam, cheating (including engine assistance in a human-vs-human game), or attempting to disrupt the service for other players is not allowed and may result in your account being suspended or removed.

You can report a player from their Profile page, from a direct-message conversation, or from a game's chat panel (the "Report opponent" button), and block a player from the same places. Blocking ends any friendship and stops that player messaging you, challenging you, or sending you friend requests; your Profile page lists the players you have blocked so you can unblock them. Reports are read by a person, and we act on them - from removing an offending bio or avatar to suspending the account. We aim to review reports within a few days, but this is a one-person project, so please email us if something is urgent.

No warranty

This service is provided "as is," with no guarantee of uptime, data preservation, or fitness for any particular purpose. In-progress games and other data may occasionally be lost during development (for example, a server restart can interrupt a live game).

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and password are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (a Google Cloud service) - RampartChess's own servers never see or store your password.
  • Profile info: the username you choose, plus an optional bio, avatar image, and country. The country is simply whatever you pick from a list - it is never taken from your device's location.
  • Gameplay data: move history, chat messages sent in a game, ratings, badges, and match results, for players in a human-vs-human game. Games you play against the computer in the Android app run entirely on your device.
  • Social data: your friend connections (who you're friends with) and any direct messages you send to a friend. Direct messages are only ever readable by the two people in that conversation.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report someone, we store your report (who reported whom, the reason, any note you add, and a copy of the reported player's recent messages or profile text so it can't be edited away before we look). Your list of blocked players is stored so the blocks can be enforced. Nobody is told who reported or blocked them.
  • Technical data: like any online service, our servers see the IP address of each request. We use it only for rate limiting (blocking abuse) and do not store it in your account or profile. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and the Android app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

How it's used

Solely to run the game itself: authenticating you, displaying your profile and match history to other players (your username, bio, avatar, rating, and public game history are visible to anyone), matching you with opponents, and keeping a record of games played.

We also send you email when something needs your attention: an email-verification message, and notifications when another player sends you a friend request, challenges you to a game, or sends you a direct message. These include the sender's username (and, for a direct message, an excerpt of it). There is not yet a setting to switch these notifications off - email us at the address under Contact and we will turn them off for you, or you can delete your account. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Account and game data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, and Storage) on Google Cloud infrastructure. The services below process data on our behalf, only to run RampartChess, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Google Firebase - sign-in, database, and avatar storage.
  • Fly.io - hosts the game server (the API that runs online games and stores results in Firebase).
  • Cloudflare - serves the rampartchess.com website and delivers its pages.
  • Resend - sends the emails described above, so it receives your email address and the content of those emails.

The rulebook shown on the Game Rules page is loaded from apecrank.net, a site run by the same developer.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself at any time from the bottom of your Profile page, in the website or the app, or email us if you cannot sign in - see how account deletion works for exactly what is removed (your sign-in account and email, profile, avatar, friends, messages, ratings, and games against the computer) and what is kept: finished games between two people stay in the other player's history, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and nothing links them back to you. Reports you filed or that were filed about you are kept for moderation records, with your name and any copied content removed. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Children's privacy

RampartChess is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

This policy may change as the project develops. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.
