Rampart Chess is a strategic hybrid of chess and card play. Two armies face each other across a rampart barrier, and victory comes by checkmating the enemy king - using both tactical piece movement and a deck of cards.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Pieces move like in chess, with a twist: Raiders (your pawns) step one square in any direction, capture diagonally, and can never cross back over the rampart barrier.
- Your deck of cards is a second set of weapons. Cast by combining exactly three cards - from your deck and from the board - that total 21.
- Raise: with a Raider inside the enemy's Jack's House, spend board cards which hold Raiders, and deck cards, to bring a captured Raider or your Queen back onto the board.
- Strike: with a Raider inside the enemy's Jack's House, capture an enemy Raider by making 21.
- The Jack, Queen and King houses on the first and last rows each unlock different powers once Raiders occupy them.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Play against the computer on your device at Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty. Games against the computer run entirely on your phone.
- Challenge other players online, with time controls from 30 minutes to a day per move.
- Chat during games, add friends and send them direct messages.
- Build your rating and earn badges and trophies.
- Review any game move by move.
- Choose your board theme and piece style.
